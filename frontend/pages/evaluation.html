ℹ️ About These Metrics

      
    
        
  • Recall@K — Fraction of relevant chunks appearing in the top-K results. Denominator: total relevant chunks in ground truth.
    • 
        
  • Precision@K — Fraction of top-K retrieved chunks that are relevant. Denominator: K.
    • 
        
  • Hit Rate@K — Whether any relevant chunk appears in top-K (1.0 = yes, 0.0 = no), averaged over cases.
    • 
        
  • MRR — Mean Reciprocal Rank of the first relevant result across cases.
    • 
        
  • nDCG@K — Normalized Discounted Cumulative Gain; accounts for ranking position of relevant chunks.
    • 
        
  • Citation Resolution Rate — Fraction of citation IDs that resolve to actual retrieved source records.
    • 
        
  • Abstention Rate — Fraction of unsupported-question cases where the tutor correctly declines to answer.
    • 
        
  • Valid Question Rate — Fraction of generated questions passing structural validation.
    • 
        
  • Quality Gates — Hard thresholds on critical metrics. A failed gate indicates a release-blocking regression.
    • 
        
  • ⚠️ Benchmark performance on synthetic data does not guarantee real-world educational effectiveness. Metrics marked preliminary are based on ≤5 cases.
    • 
        
  • Metrics unavailable due to missing components are shown as N/A, not zero.
